// 05 · research frontiers

Where he is pushing.

Human–robot collaboration

Safety and shared workspaces, so a cobot and a person can work the same task without a fence between them.

AMR navigation & fleets

Autonomous navigation and multi-robot orchestration for factories that move material without a human driver.

Generative visuomotor systems

Models that see and then act, connecting perception straight to motion for manipulation and mobility.

Edge AI

Embedded intelligence that senses, decides and acts on the device, for robots and for energy infrastructure.

Digital twins & sim-to-real

Simulate first, deploy second: digital-twin workflows and pipelines that carry results from simulation onto real hardware.

Energy systems

Renewable energy, photovoltaics and energy analytics, with optimisation aimed at systems that last.
